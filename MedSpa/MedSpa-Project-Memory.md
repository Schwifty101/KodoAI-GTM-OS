## Account and buyer

Target active US or Canadian med spas, day spas, wellness spas, cosmetic dermatology practices and aesthetics groups with a material relevant service line.

The buyer must control communications, booking, data or integrations. Qualifying roles include founder, co-founder, owner, CEO, president, COO, executive director and owner medical director. A location manager without stack authority does not qualify.

One Touch 1 per company: when a lead list surfaces several contacts at the same company, research them all, pick the single best-qualifying executive-level contact and draft to that person only. If no contact at the company reaches the executive-level bar (for example only a CFO or a VP of Finance, HR or Marketing surfaces), skip the company.

Stop for closed or rebranded businesses, minor aesthetics add-ons, plastic-surgery-only businesses, unrelated salons or wellness businesses, uninspectable journeys, DNC, explicit opt-outs or bounces.

Standing source-file disqualifications: Fibroblast Treatment Utah; Esthetics by Emi Med Spa; CoCo Art & Spa; Enliven Medical; Modern Med Spa. The recorded reason is failure to find a qualifying buyer contact. Do not use a non-qualifying contact to bypass this.

Record exact employee and location counts when available. Never estimate them. Missing headcount does not select a vector or create a size-based claim.

## Evidence and routing

Evaluate every prospect through five fields:

1. Current Signal: a dated public change or still-live condition.
2. Likely Objective: the specific project the buyer may be trying to accomplish.
3. Relevant Handoff: one directly observed public workflow.
4. Reason to Respond: one internal sentence explaining this business's relevance.
5. Evidence Strength: field-level classification as Observed fact, Reasonable inference or Unknown.

Signal-led Touch 1 requires an observed current signal and observed handoff, a coherent objective, a specific reason to respond and no material platform contradiction.

When no current signal reaches Observed fact, record Current Signal as Unknown and evaluate relevance-led Touch 1. It requires an observed handoff, a business-specific reason to respond, an inferred objective tested directly in the final question, and no material platform contradiction.

Hold if neither gate survives. A permanent form or phone number alone is not demand. A public signal does not establish an internal priority. Unknowns never appear as claims in the email. The lane derives from Current Signal's evidence classification; do not add a separate Notion lane field.

## Three active vectors

| Vector | Evidence needed for the handoff |
|---|---|
| Conversion recovery | Visible inquiry-to-booking handoff |
| Capacity | Repetitive booking, follow-up, call-handling or channel-reconciliation work visibly assigned across people or systems. An after-hours call handoff also qualifies when the phone-dependent task demonstrably stops or degrades outside a verified coverage window, without an equivalent 24/7 route |
| Rebooking and retention | Visible manual or fragmented recall route tied to a real return cadence |

The signal-led lane also needs a directly connected current event. Membership growth is not a standalone vector; a membership launch can support conversion or rebooking and retention. After-hours call coverage and Cancellation recovery are retired as standalone vectors as of October 10, 2026. A cancellation or waitlist gap can be a handoff only under Capacity or Rebooking and retention after native-platform screening.

Inspect websites, booking, forms, landing pages, chat, Instagram/Facebook routes, text, WhatsApp, Linktree, Google messaging, location-specific destinations and public phone coverage. Never submit forms, enter personal details, call, book or mystery-shop.

A vendor advertising a feature does not prove the clinic enabled it. A visibly working native route blocks the overlapping angle. Check the exact workflow and current vendor capabilities during prospect research. A Zenoti or PatientNow label alone neither proves a gap nor disqualifies the account. Position surviving custom work as a layer over the existing system. Do not promise clinical judgment.

Use Voice Agent labels conservatively: Confirmed, Possible, Human-led or No Evidence. No Evidence does not mean no voice agent exists. Posted hours alone do not prove calls go unanswered.

## Writing and sequence

Use US English, simple conversational language, no em dashes, no generic compliments, fake familiarity or jargon. Use “We build custom AI systems...” for KodoAI's capability, while Soban's introduction can use “I'm.”

| Touch | Current approved behavior |
|---|---|
| Touch 1, Day 0 | 45–70 words excluding subject and 5 to 8 sentences in the body; introduction and credibility → signal → handoff → how we can help → one priority question. Relevance-led drops the signal sentence. |
| Touch 2, Day 3 | Same-thread bump; 10–35 words excluding greeting/signature; repeat only the exact objective from Touch 1 and end with one question. |
| Touch 3, Day 7 | Same-thread partner-fit framing and one priority question about the original objective; 15–40 words excluding greeting/signature; no new prospect claim, number or offer. |

Touch 1 offers no meeting, call, trial or asset and includes no modeled results or financial figures. Its relevance-led closing question has no soft opt-out. Touch 2 explicitly permits “or is it not a priority?” This difference is intentional across touches, not a reason to add that phrase to Touch 1.

Follow-ups carry no planning model, number, new research, proof point, diagnosis, competitor comparison, trial, audit, asset, call, meeting or walkthrough. Touch 3 may add one honest partner-fit line; it does not add a new benefit argument. Do not re-research just to write a follow-up. The approved sequence has no Touch 4. Every email ends with exactly one question.

### Touch 1 five rules and credibility

Touch 1 follows Soban's five rules: (1) 5 to 8 sentences, max; (2) lead with who you are and why you're worth talking to; (3) personalize, but stay on topic; (4) explain in 1-2 sentences how you think you can help; (5) write like a founder, not an AE. Order: “I'm Soban, founder of KodoAI.” plus one sentence naming clients, then the signal and handoff, then how we can help, then one question. The 45 to 70 word limit applies (greeting and signature included); five short sentences is the practical shape.

Credibility: Touch 1 does not mention Bluegile. It says “We've worked with Rolls-Royce, Saudi Aramco and Patagonia” (two or three names), each backed by a case study on Bluegile's portfolio, which is an internal source only. Never imply a med-spa client, a named company using KodoAI, results, or logo-only brands (Patreon, Nutrabolt, Jazzcash) without Soban's confirmation for that send. The approved list and full limits are in the Touch 1 prompt, section 8b.

These are KodoAI's approved choices. Rob's supplied article supports a three-day email bump; it does not establish KodoAI's exact objective-question bump, partner-fit Touch 3, cadence limit or word counts.

## Standing decisions

- Relevance-led outreach is a standing fallback when no current online signal is observed; the full observed-handoff gate still applies.
- A client case is not required before outreach. Market evidence can justify testing an offer, while the buyer's internal priority stays unconfirmed until the buyer supplies evidence.
- Use “prospect relevance case” for the public-research analysis.
- Touch 3 uses this template:

```text
[Name],

We're choosing med-spa partners who are ready to make [objective tested in Touch 1] a priority this quarter.

Is that where [Company] is right now?

Soban
```

- Selectivity is tied to readiness to prioritize the work. Do not imply existing clients, limited slots or a quarterly capacity constraint. A “select partners each quarter” availability claim requires confirmation of a real policy.
- The priority-first CTA, word limits, Day 0/3/7 timing and approval/logging boundaries stand. Exactly one question ends each email.
- Three active Touch 1 vectors: Conversion recovery, Capacity (including after-hours call handling) and Rebooking and retention (including membership). After-hours call coverage and Cancellation recovery are not standalone vectors. A cancellation or waitlist gap can be a handoff only under Capacity or Rebooking and retention, after native-platform screening shows the refill mechanism absent, disabled or failing.
- Basis for the three vectors: the October 10 vector review (https://claude.ai/artifact/VG5mRqiE2UA9wJrgubQRPP). It found no vector has campaign evidence yet (0 positive or neutral replies across 186 prospects through October 7), so the three vectors are a hypothesis to test, not a proven ranking.
- Test plan, accepted by Soban: about 50 delivered prospects per vector, positive or neutral replies as the success measure, and every “stop” reply logged separately with its touch number.
- The Batch02 Vector Used dropdown keeps the retired options because historical rows use them. Do not select them and do not remove them. Batch03 lists only the three active options and has no Gap Verified or Touch 1 Number property.
- Batch03 (Med Spa Prospects - Batch03) is the live prospect database on the Med Spa Cold Email Tracker page. All new prospect profiles go there. Batch02 takes no new profiles but keeps its rows for Touch 2, Touch 3 and confirmed resends. Batch01 stays a read-only archive. Search all three for duplicates and Do Not Contact before researching.
- Campaign Batch convention for Batch03: the value is exactly `Batch03` on every new row, with no spaces, regions, list names or dates (those live in State, HQ City, Source, Notes and Created). Batch02 values are left unchanged.
- When Soban corrects a fact, acknowledge it, recheck the source or thread, update evidence/Notes when authorized, and apply the correction to remaining leads. Do not preserve a failed angle to finish a batch.
