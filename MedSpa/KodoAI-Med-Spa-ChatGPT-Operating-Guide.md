## 2. The governing theory

**A public workflow gap is not demand.** A signal-led prospect qualifies when research finds a current situation that plausibly creates a project now. A relevance-led prospect does not claim demand; it qualifies only when a real observed handoff gives KodoAI a business-specific reason to test priority directly. A static form, phone number or awkward booking page is insufficient by itself unless it meets that relevance-led gate.

Every send must answer five questions:

1. **Current signal:** What changed recently?
2. **Likely objective:** What are they probably trying to accomplish?
3. **Relevant handoff:** What observable workflow could obstruct that objective?
4. **Reason to respond:** Why would this founder care about hearing from KodoAI now?
5. **Evidence strength:** Which parts are observed facts, reasonable inferences or unknown?

For signal-led Touch 1, the current signal and relevant handoff must both be observed and sourced. For relevance-led Touch 1, Current Signal is recorded as Unknown while the relevant handoff must still be observed and sourced. The likely objective is normally a reasonable inference and must be tested by the closing question. Unknowns never appear in the email.

**No observed current signal, no signal-led Touch 1.** Do not rescue a weak lead with a competitor comparison or a modeled number.

No current signal means no signal-led Touch 1, not automatically no outreach: see the relevance-led fallback in section 6 for a lead with a real observed handoff but no dated signal (full gate detail lives in Med-Spa-Touch-1-Research-Master-Prompt.md section 4.5). Missing online signals do not establish that nothing is happening internally. The relevance-led fallback is a standing route, not an exception to remove.


### Market strategy before KodoAI has its own client examples

Soban is testing an offering in a category where other providers' work may show a viable opportunity. An existing KodoAI client or case study is not required to research or approach a qualifying prospect. Soban's team has worked with major enterprises, and Touch 1 names a few as its credibility line without mentioning Bluegile. That line is not demand proof and not med-spa experience.

Use sourced category evidence to choose which workflows and capabilities to test. This market-level evidence does not establish one clinic's internal priority, need to switch or intent to buy. The email tests that priority. Keep public observations, inferred objectives and buyer-confirmed facts separate.

Rob's [Waves, dams, and rivers](https://thephysicsofstartups.substack.com/p/waves-dams-and-rivers) is supporting strategy context. Refer to `KodoAI-Cold-Outreach-Writing-Rules.md` for the attribution and evidence limits. Category research is separate from the optional prospect-specific competitor analysis below.

## 3. Active Touch 1 vectors

There are three active Touch 1 vectors.

| Vector | Signal-led current situation | Relevant handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion recovery | New advertising, treatment launch, lead source, booking route, provider or location creating new inbound demand | An observed inquiry-to-booking handoff tied to that change |
| Capacity | Live hiring (for example a patient coordinator or front-desk vacancy), overtime, outsourced cover, a new site, stated expansion, an expanded coverage need or a current evening/weekend demand signal | The repetitive booking, follow-up, call-handling or channel-reconciliation work attached to that situation. A call route that demonstrably stops or degrades outside a verified window, with no equivalent 24/7 route, also qualifies |
| Rebooking and retention | New treatment program, membership, provider or service line with an obvious return cadence | A visible manual or fragmented recall route tied to that program |

Retired as standalone Touch 1 vectors:

- **After-hours call coverage.** It overlaps native voice receptionists on the major platforms and is now an observed handoff under Capacity. The after-hours evidence rules still apply to any email that mentions calls: posted office hours are not proof that calls go unanswered, verify what happens outside the window, rule out a 24/7 human, answering service, voice agent or equivalent self-service route, and never claim one is absent without public evidence.
- **Cancellation recovery.** It overlaps native waitlist features and, per the October 10 vector review, has a small recoverable pool. A cancellation or waitlist gap may appear as the handoff under Capacity or Rebooking and retention only when native-platform screening shows the refill mechanism absent, disabled or failing.
- **Capacity without hiring** and **Membership growth** were already retired. A membership launch may supply a current signal for conversion or for rebooking and retention when the public handoff supports it.

Rebooking and retention is expected to run relevance-led more often than the other two, because dated signals for it are rare. The lane is still derived only from Current Signal's Evidence Strength.

For relevance-led Touch 1, a current situation is not required. Select one of the three active vectors from the observed handoff, apply the same native-platform and contradiction screening, and hold when the handoff itself cannot reach Observed fact.

## 5. End-to-end Touch 1 run

### Stage 1: duplicate and DNC check

Search Batch03, Batch02 and the Batch 01 archive before researching. Stop immediately if **Do Not Contact** is checked. Do not repeat a completed sequence unless Soban explicitly starts a new one.

### Stage 2: qualify the account and buyer

The business must be a trading US or Canadian med spa, day spa, wellness spa, cosmetic dermatology practice or aesthetics group where the relevant service line is material. The buyer must control communications, booking, data or integrations: founder, co-founder, owner, CEO, president, COO, executive director or an owner medical director.

**One Touch 1 per company.** If a lead list surfaces several contacts at the same company, research them all, pick the single best-qualifying executive-level contact and draft to that person only. If no contact at the company reaches the executive-level bar (for example only a CFO or a VP of Finance, HR or Marketing surfaces), skip the company. Do not run it.

Standing disqualifications: **Fibroblast Treatment Utah, Esthetics by Emi Med Spa, CoCo Art & Spa, Enliven Medical, and Modern Med Spa**. Apollo, Vibe Prospecting, Phantom Buster and manual research found no qualifying buyer contact. Do not draft to a non-qualifying contact at any of them.

### Stage 3: verify the current signal

Record the source, date and whether the signal is still live. Good signals include a live job post, dated expansion, new location, new provider, new service or device, new campaign, new operating hours, acquisition, platform migration or a competitor move that changes the local market.

A permanent website feature, ordinary opening hours, a generic service menu or an old announcement is not enough.

If no signal reaches Observed fact, record Current Signal as Unknown and evaluate the relevance-led gate. Do not hold solely because the signal is missing.

### Stage 4: form the prospect relevance case

Write the likely objective in plain language. Then identify one public handoff directly connected to it. For signal-led Touch 1, the signal, objective and handoff must form one coherent chain. If they do not, evaluate whether the observed handoff independently meets the relevance-led gate; otherwise hold the lead.

### Stage 5: screen the vector

Inspect the public website, booking platform, social booking routes, phone route, text, WhatsApp, Linktree, Google messaging, forms, landing pages and current hiring or expansion signals. Never submit, call, book or mystery-shop.

Screen native capabilities before selecting a vector. A platform selling a feature does not prove the clinic enabled it, but a visibly working native route blocks the overlapping angle.

Zenoti AI Plus or AI Workforce being live does not disqualify a prospect by itself. Walk only when the current evidence shows the exact bottleneck in question is already covered end to end: clinical triage or screening, Instagram DM handling, multi-constraint device/room/provider scheduling, or cross-platform continuity. Otherwise position KodoAI as a custom layer sitting on top of Zenoti, never as a replacement. Apply the same logic to PatientNow after checking PatientNow's own current AI and automation capabilities for the exact workflow.

### Stage 6: competitor conditional

Competitor research is never mandatory. Use it only when the competitor's recent action is itself part of the current situation: for example, a directly overlapping clinic launches a new location, service, campaign, booking route or 24/7 coverage that creates present market pressure.

Do not use static parity, treatment-menu overlap or "they book better" as the reason to send. A competitor name appears in the email only when naming it materially clarifies the current situation without shaming the prospect. Otherwise keep it internal or omit it.

### Stage 7: draft and present

Use the single master template below. The email must follow the five rules in section 6: 45 to 70 words excluding subject, 5 to 8 sentences in the body, an introduction with one named-client credibility sentence first, US English, no em dash and one question at the end. Show it in an editable email writing block. State that nothing has been sent and Notion is unchanged.

### Stage 8: send and log

Nothing is sent without Soban's explicit approval. After he confirms the send, record the exact subject and edited body in Batch03, set **Campaign Batch** to exactly `Batch03` (no other wording), set **Status = Sent**, **Touch = 1**, and record the actual send date. Then set **Next Follow-ups = send date + 3 days** yourself. Batch03 has no Notion automations, so it never updates on its own.

## 6. Master Touch 1 template

**Subject:** `[Company] / [current situation]`

```text
[Name],

I'm Soban, founder of KodoAI. We've worked with [two or three names from the approved client list].

[Company] [verified, time-bound signal]. [Specific customer or operational task] still [observed handoff stated without interpretation].

We build custom AI systems around [vector-specific outcome]. [Optional, only if the email stays within 70 words: I think we could [how that applies to the observed handoff].]

Is [specific objective connected to the signal] something you're actively working on this quarter?

Soban | Founder, KodoAI
```

Vector-specific outcome lines:

| Vector | Wording |
|---|---|
| Conversion recovery | the handoff between an inquiry landing and someone actually booking |
| Capacity | keeping rising booking and follow-up volume from becoming more front-desk work |
| Capacity (only for a verified after-hours route failure) | handling and booking calls that land after the front desk closes |
| Rebooking and retention | bringing due clients back on treatment cadence without manual chasing |

### Relevance-led fallback

Used only when no current signal can be established as an observed fact but the relevant handoff still is (full gate in Med-Spa-Touch-1-Research-Master-Prompt.md section 4.5). Same word limit, sentence range and hard rules as above. The opening states the handoff only; the closing question tests priority directly with no soft opt-out ("or not right now", "no worries if not", or a hedge sentence before the question are all banned).

```text
[Name],

I'm Soban, founder of KodoAI. We've worked with [two or three names from the approved client list].

[Company]'s [observed handoff, stated as fact, no interpretation].

We build custom AI systems around [vector-specific outcome]. [Optional, only if the email stays within 70 words: I think we could [how that applies to the observed handoff].]

Is [objective tied to that handoff] something you're actively working on right now?

Soban | Founder, KodoAI
```

### Five rules and credibility line for Touch 1

Source: Soban's reference “5 Rules for Good Cold Emails,” adapted for KodoAI. These govern the order and length of Touch 1 only. Every evidence, claim and offer restriction elsewhere still applies. The approved client names and the case study behind each one are in `Med-Spa-Touch-1-Research-Master-Prompt.md`, section 8b.

| # | Rule | How it applies to a KodoAI Touch 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 to 8 sentences, max | Count the body only, not the subject, greeting or signature; the question counts. The Touch 1 limit of 45 to 70 words excluding subject (greeting and signature included) also applies and binds first, so write about five short sentences of roughly 12 words each. |
| 2 | Lead with who you are and why you're worth talking to | Open with “I'm Soban, founder of KodoAI.” and one sentence naming enterprise clients, such as “We've worked with Rolls-Royce, Saudi Aramco and Patagonia.” Do not mention Bluegile. The prospect's signal comes second. |
| 3 | Personalize. But stay on topic! | One sentence, two at most: the observed signal and the observed handoff. No compliment, rapport detail or other fact about the business. |
| 4 | Explain in 1-2 sentences how you think you can help them | “We build custom AI systems around [vector-specific outcome].” Add a second sentence beginning “I think we could...” only if the email stays within 70 words. No numbers, guarantees, savings or claims about unobserved performance. |
| 5 | Write like a founder, not an AE | First person, plain words, short sentences, contractions allowed. One question and no meeting or demo ask. Avoid “I wanted to reach out,” “I hope this finds you well,” “touch base,” “circle back,” “quick question,” “reach out,” “synergy,” “leverage,” “best-in-class,” “cutting-edge,” “game-changing,” “unlock,” “empower,” “seamless,” “turnkey,” “pain points” and “solutions” as a vague noun. |

Word budget for the whole email, excluding subject: greeting 1, introduction 5, credibility about 9, signal and handoff about 15 to 20, how we can help about 15, question about 15, signature 4. The relevance-led lane drops the signal and states the handoff only.

#### Credibility line (named clients)

Touch 1 does not mention Bluegile (Soban's decision). Credibility is one sentence naming two or three enterprise clients, such as “We've worked with Rolls-Royce, Saudi Aramco and Patagonia.” Names come only from the approved list in the Touch 1 prompt, and each must appear in a case study on Bluegile's published portfolio. The email never cites that portfolio.

Never state or imply:

- a med-spa, aesthetics or healthcare client;
- that any named company uses, endorses or referred KodoAI. Say “we've worked with,” never “our customers,” “trusted by” or “clients include”;
- what the work was beyond what the case study says, or any result, saving, rating or client count;
- names that appear on bluegile.xyz only as logos (Patreon, Nutrabolt, Jazzcash), unless Soban confirms them in chat for that send;
- SOC2 or other compliance wording, or any pricing.

If a prospect asks, answer plainly that the work was done through Soban's parent company, Bluegile Technologies.

## 8. Failure modes

1. Sending from a static website detail that does not meet the relevance-led minimum gate.
2. Treating a signal as proof of the buyer's internal problem or priority.
3. Writing a competitor comparison merely to demonstrate research.
4. Using modeled treatments, slots, memberships, hours or dollars in Touch 1.
5. Letting the handoff drift away from the current signal in a signal-led email.
6. Putting a reasonable inference into the email as an observed fact.
7. Using posted office hours as proof that calls go unanswered.
8. Using a cancellation or waitlist gap as the handoff without showing the native waitlist is absent, disabled or failing. Cancellation recovery is no longer a standalone vector.
9. Filling a bracket with an unsupported assumption to finish the draft.
10. Drafting to more than one contact at the same company, or to a non-executive contact when no executive-level contact qualifies.
11. Selecting a retired vector (After-hours call coverage, Cancellation recovery, Capacity without hiring or Membership growth) for a new row.
12. Opening Touch 1 with the prospect's signal instead of who Soban is and why he is worth talking to.
13. Going past 70 words or eight sentences, or adding rapport detail that is not the signal or the handoff.
14. Naming Bluegile in Touch 1, or a credibility claim outside the approved named-client list: a result, a logo without case-study text, a med-spa client, or a named company implied to be a KodoAI customer.
15. AE phrasing such as “I wanted to reach out,” “touch base,” “solutions” or “leverage,” or a meeting or demo ask.

## 9. Hard rules

- US English; no em dashes.
- Exactly one question per email, at the end, including Touch 3.
- Touch 1 follows the five rules: 45 to 70 words, introduction and one named-client credibility sentence first, 5 to 8 sentences, one or two sentences on how we think we can help, founder voice.
- Credibility comes only from the approved named-client list, and Touch 1 does not mention Bluegile. Never imply a med-spa client base, a named company using KodoAI, results, or logo-only brands.
- No Touch 1 modeled performance or financial figures.
- No meeting, call, trial or asset offer in Touch 1.
- No generic compliments, fake familiarity or jargon. A slightly conversational tone is preferred, and one "I noticed" in the opening signal sentence is acceptable, provided every fact stated stays observed and sourced.
- No clinical judgment, diagnosis, candidacy assessment or unverified compliance claim.
- Never claim a voice agent or answering service is absent without public evidence.
- Never submit a form, call, book or mystery-shop.
- Process one prospect at a time unless Soban requests a batch.
- Preserve historical outreach; confirmed resends are appended as new history, not treated as if the earlier send never happened.

## 10. Follow-up boundary

Touch 1 establishes a current situation and relevant handoff when signal-led, or an observed handoff and tested priority when relevance-led. It no longer supplies a number for Touch 2 to repeat. The follow-up prompt must pull the signal, objective, handoff, evidence strength, vector and exact Touch 1 body.

The annual planning model is removed. Touches 2 and 3 carry no planning model, modeled figure or number of any kind. Touch 2 is a plain same-thread bump. Touch 3 uses one honest partner-fit line and one question about the same objective, as set out in `Med-Spa-Follow-Up-Master-Prompt.md`. The five-rule sentence range and the credibility line belong to Touch 1 only; Touches 2 and 3 stay short bumps in the same founder voice.

Touch 3 replaces the former close-off. It uses 15 to 40 words excluding greeting and signature, exactly one question at the end, and no new prospect claim, research, proof point, diagnosis or offer. Selectivity is tied to the buyer being ready to prioritize the work. Do not imply existing med-spa clients, limited slots, a full calendar, deadlines or a quarterly capacity limit without a real, confirmed basis.

The exact Touch 2 and Touch 3 templates, Day 0/3/7 cadence and word limits are KodoAI decisions. Rob's supplied article supports an email bump after three days; it does not prescribe this complete sequence.

Rows tagged with a retired vector complete their cadence unchanged. Touch 2 and Touch 3 carry the objective already tested in Touch 1, so the vector label does not change the follow-up.
