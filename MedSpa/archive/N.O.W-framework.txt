# The NOW Framework (Sales Messaging)

The goal of this framework is **not to convince people to buy**. The goal is to make the cost of staying the same feel greater than the cost of changing.

---

# N — Name the Pain

People don't buy because they have a problem.

They buy because they have a **specific, painful, emotionally relevant problem**.

**Vague problems don't sell. Specific pain does.**

### ❌ Bad
> Do you want to grow your business?

### ✅ Good
> You're stuck at $500k because you're still the only salesperson, and every month your revenue depends entirely on you.

The more specific the pain, the more the prospect sees themselves in it.

**Rule:** If they don't feel the pain, they won't fix it.

---

# O — Open the Gap

Show the difference between:

- **Current State** (where they are today)
- **Future State** (where they could be)

Increase the perceived distance between these two realities.

### Current State
- Slow
- Manual
- Founder-dependent
- Constant firefighting
- No predictable growth

### Future State
- Systems run without you
- A trained sales team closes deals
- Predictable revenue
- Freedom to focus on growth

The bigger this gap appears, the more valuable your solution becomes.

> **Rule:** Bigger gap = Bigger perceived value = Higher price tolerance.

---

# W — Withdraw the Path

This is where most people get sales wrong.

They:
- Oversell
- Chase prospects
- Follow up endlessly
- Try to convince

Instead, become selective.

Position your offer as being for people who are ready to solve the problem **now**.

### Example

> I only work with founders who are serious about fixing this problem right now.

> If you're not ready yet, we're probably not the right fit.

Scarcity and selectivity increase perceived value.

People naturally want what feels difficult to access.

---

# The Formula

```text
Specific Pain
      ↓
Show the Gap
      ↓
Withdraw Access
      ↓
Create Urgency
      ↓
Higher Conversions
```

Or as an equation:

```text
The bigger the pain
+ The bigger the gap
+ The more you withdraw access
= The more urgency you create
= The more you sell.
```

# LLM Prompt

## Task

Rewrite the following sales copy using the **NOW Framework**.

### Step 1 — Name the Pain
- Replace vague statements with a highly specific, emotionally relevant pain point.
- Make the reader immediately think, "That's exactly my situation."
- Use concrete examples, numbers, consequences, or frustrations instead of generic language.

### Step 2 — Open the Gap
- Contrast the prospect's **current state** with their **desired future state**.
- Make the transformation feel significant and valuable.
- Increase the perceived cost of staying where they are.

### Step 3 — Withdraw the Path
- Do **not** sound needy or desperate.
- Do **not** oversell.
- Position the offer as being intended only for people who are genuinely ready to solve the problem now.
- Create selectivity rather than pressure.

## Writing Style

- Conversational
- Direct
- Short sentences
- Plain English
- Confident, not arrogant
- Psychologically compelling
- No hype
- No exaggerated claims
- No corporate jargon
- Make the reader feel understood rather than sold to.

## Output Format

Return:

1. **Name the Pain**
2. **Open the Gap**
3. **Withdraw the Path**
4. **Final rewritten sales copy**
