## 0. Governing change

Touch 1 uses a signal-led lane first and a relevance-led fallback when no current signal reaches Observed fact.

**No observed current signal, no signal-led Touch 1.** A static website gap, ordinary opening hours, an awkward form or a competitor with easier booking does not establish demand.

No current signal means no signal-led Touch 1. It does not by itself mean no outreach: see the relevance-led minimum send gate in section 4.5 and the fallback template in section 8a, for a lead with a real observed handoff but no dated signal.

Retired from Touch 1:

- `Med-Spa-Touch-1-Research-Master-Prompt-Lean.md`, `N.O.W-framework.txt`, `The-KodoAI-Med-Spa-Outreach-Protocol.txt`, and all competitor-first structures.
- Mandatory competitor research or a required competitor sentence.
- Modeled treatments, appointments, slots, memberships, hours or money.
- Tier-based Touch 1 figures and the `about 1 in 3` missed-call benchmark.
- Competitor-gap maps, audits, assets, free trials, meeting asks and call asks.
- Accumulated-loss framing and any claim about unobserved clinic performance.
- "80% fewer manual hours," "recover 50%," unsupported "without adding headcount," compliance claims, and clinical judgment.

The goal is to show one observed public handoff, connect it to KodoAI's relevant capability, and ask whether the related objective is active. Include a current situation when it is publicly verified. Web research cannot establish every internal initiative; missing online signals alone do not block the relevance-led lane.

Soban does not have client examples to ground this campaign's demand assumptions. An existing KodoAI client, case study or buyer-confirmed internal project is not a prerequisite for cold outreach. Sourced evidence that other providers are serving a workflow can guide market and offer selection. It cannot be presented as proof that this individual prospect has an active project or needs to switch.

Soban's team has worked with major enterprises, verified against Bluegile's published portfolio. Touch 1 names a few of them as its credibility line (section 8b) and does not mention Bluegile. That line shows who Soban's team is. It is not demand proof, not med-spa experience, and never a reason to imply a med-spa client base.

Call the internal analysis a **prospect relevance case**, not confirmed demand. Test the inferred priority directly in the email. Market-level evidence belongs in internal research notes when relevant; it creates no new mandatory field or send gate. It does not make prospect-specific competitor research mandatory.

## 3. Qualify the account and buyer

The business must be trading in the US or Canada and fit one of these categories: med spa, day spa, wellness spa, cosmetic dermatology or an aesthetics group where the relevant service line is material.

The buyer must control communications, booking, data or integrations: founder, co-founder, owner, CEO, president, COO, executive director or an owner medical director.

**One Touch 1 per company.** If a lead list surfaces several contacts at the same company, research them all, pick the single best-qualifying executive-level contact and draft to that person only. If no contact at the company reaches the executive-level bar (for example only a CFO or a VP of Finance, HR or Marketing surfaces), skip the company. Do not run it.

Hard stops:

- Closed or rebranded business.
- Aesthetics is a minor add-on.
- Plastic-surgery-only, unrelated salon or unrelated wellness business.
- Franchise-local or location-level contact without authority over the stack.
- No public evidence with which to inspect the relevant journey.

Standing account disqualifications: **Fibroblast Treatment Utah, Esthetics by Emi Med Spa, CoCo Art & Spa, Enliven Medical, and Modern Med Spa**. Apollo, Vibe Prospecting, Phantom Buster and manual research found no qualifying buyer contact. Do not draft to a non-qualifying contact at any of them.

## 4. Build the prospect relevance case

Every prospect must be evaluated through the following five fields.

### 4.1 Current Signal: What changed recently?

Find one time-bound public event or condition that is still relevant now. Record the exact source and date.

Examples:

- Live front-desk, coordinator or operations hiring.
- A dated new location, provider, room, device or service launch.
- New advertising, promotion, lead source or landing page.
- New or extended operating hours.
- Acquisition, ownership change, centralization or platform migration.
- A newly launched membership or treatment program.
- A competitor's recent action that materially changes the local market.

Not sufficient alone:

- A permanent contact form.
- Ordinary published hours.
- A general service menu.
- An undated "coming soon" statement.
- A competitor with a cleaner booking journey.
- A website element that may have existed for years.

If recency or present relevance cannot be established, classify the signal as **Unknown** and evaluate the relevance-led minimum gate in section 4.5. Hold only when the Relevant Handoff also fails to reach Observed fact or another part of that gate fails.

### 4.2 Likely Objective: What are they probably trying to accomplish?

Write one plain-language objective connected to the signal in the signal-led lane, or to the observed handoff in the relevance-led lane. Examples: absorb a new location's demand, reduce the workload attached to an active hire, fill new provider capacity, keep a multi-location journey consistent, or support a new treatment's repeat cadence.

The likely objective is usually a **Reasonable inference**, not an observed fact. Never state it in the email as if the buyer confirmed it. The closing question tests it.

### 4.3 Relevant Handoff: What observable workflow could obstruct that objective?

Walk the public journey as far as possible without submitting anything. Inspect:

1. Website booking and inquiry routes.
2. Instagram and Facebook DMs, text, WhatsApp, Linktree and Google messaging.
3. Phone-dependent tasks, published coverage and public after-hours routing.
4. Landing pages, forms and chat widgets outside the main booking platform.
5. Location-specific numbers, inboxes, calendars and booking destinations.
6. Hiring or expansion documents that explicitly assign repetitive work to a person.

Select one handoff directly connected to the likely objective. If the signal and handoff require two unrelated stories, do not use the signal-led lane. Evaluate the observed handoff under the relevance-led gate; otherwise hold the lead.

Never submit a form, enter personal information, call, book or mystery-shop.

### 4.4 Reason to Respond: Why would this founder care about hearing from KodoAI now?

Write one internal sentence connecting the active situation to KodoAI's relevant capability in the signal-led lane, or connecting the observed handoff to a specific KodoAI capability in the relevance-led lane. This is the message's causal theory, not another personalization field.

A valid reason to respond must be:

- Current in the signal-led lane, because the signal is active now; business-specific in the relevance-led lane, because the observed handoff is real and material enough to test priority.
- Specific, because it names the handoff affected by that signal.
- Relevant, because KodoAI can change that handoff without claiming clinical judgment.
- Honest, because it does not rely on a modeled result or an invented internal failure.

If the only reason is "we researched them" or "a competitor does it better," hold the lead.

### 4.5 Evidence Strength: Observed fact, reasonable inference or unknown

Classify every material claim:

- **Observed fact:** directly visible in a current public source. Safe to state.
- **Reasonable inference:** follows from observed facts but is not confirmed internally. May shape the closing question; do not state it as fact.
- **Unknown:** not publicly established. Never appears in the email.

Minimum send gate, signal-led:

- Current Signal = Observed fact.
- Relevant Handoff = Observed fact.
- Likely Objective = Observed fact or Reasonable inference.
- Reason to Respond = coherent and specific.
- No material contradiction from platform screening.

Minimum send gate, relevance-led (used only when Current Signal cannot reach Observed fact):

- Relevant Handoff = Observed fact.
- Likely Objective = Reasonable inference, tested by the closing question, never asserted as fact.
- Reason to Respond = specific to this business, not generic to the vector.
- No material contradiction from platform screening.

Neither gate met (Relevant Handoff also below Observed fact): hold the lead.

The lane is derived automatically from Current Signal's own Evidence Strength value above, not a separate field: Observed fact routes to signal-led; anything weaker with Relevant Handoff still Observed fact routes to relevance-led.

## 5. Select and screen the vector

There are three active Touch 1 vectors.

| Vector | Signal-led send only when |
|---|---|
| Conversion recovery | A current demand-generating event is tied to an observed inquiry-to-booking handoff |
| Capacity | A live hire, outsourced cover, overtime signal, new site, stated expansion, expanded coverage need or current evening/weekend demand signal is tied to repetitive booking, follow-up, call-handling or channel-reconciliation work. For an after-hours call handoff, the public route must also demonstrably degrade outside a verified window with no equivalent 24/7 route closing the task |
| Rebooking and retention | A current program, provider or service-line signal has a real return cadence and the public recall route is visibly manual or fragmented |

For relevance-led Touch 1, select the vector from the observed handoff rather than a current event:

| Vector | Relevance-led observed handoff required |
|---|---|
| Conversion recovery | A visible inquiry-to-booking handoff |
| Capacity | Repetitive booking, follow-up, call-handling or channel-reconciliation work visibly assigned across people or systems, or a phone-dependent task whose public route demonstrably stops or degrades outside a verified window with no equivalent 24/7 route |
| Rebooking and retention | A visible manual or fragmented recall route connected to a return cadence |

Membership growth, After-hours call coverage and Cancellation recovery are not standalone Touch 1 vectors. A membership launch may support conversion or rebooking and retention when the current signal and handoff meet the gate. A cancellation or waitlist gap may be the handoff under Capacity or Rebooking and retention only after native-platform screening shows the refill mechanism absent, disabled or failing.

Platform rules:

- A platform selling a feature does not prove the clinic enabled it.
- A visibly working native route blocks the overlapping vector.
- Zenoti, Boulevard, Vagaro and Aesthetic Record have native waitlist, deposit, reminder and membership functions. Do not pitch them as missing features.
- Zenoti AI Plus or AI Workforce being live does not disqualify a prospect by itself. Walk only when the current evidence shows the exact bottleneck in question is already covered end to end: clinical triage or screening, Instagram DM handling, multi-constraint device/room/provider scheduling, or cross-platform continuity.
- When that end-to-end contradiction is absent, position KodoAI as a custom layer sitting on top of Zenoti, never as a replacement.
- Apply the same logic to PatientNow only after checking PatientNow's own current AI and automation capabilities for the exact workflow. Do not assume overlap from the platform name alone.
- Posted hours alone do not prove after-hours failure.
- A confirmed 24/7 human, answering service, voice agent or equivalent self-service route blocks the matching after-hours angle.
- Never make the software vendor the subject of the email.

Set **Voice Agent** conservatively: `Confirmed`, `Possible`, `Human-led`, or `No Evidence`.

## 6. Competitor analysis conditional

Competitor research is optional and never required to finish Touch 1.

Use competitor activity only when all of the following are true:

1. The competitor is direct: overlapping market, audience and relevant service line.
2. The action is recent and sourced.
3. The action materially changes the prospect's current situation or likely objective.
4. The competitor is functioning as a demand signal, not as proof that the prospect is losing patients.
5. Naming the competitor genuinely clarifies why the issue matters now.

If used, keep the research internal by default. Name the competitor in the email only when doing so strengthens the current-situation sentence without shaming the buyer or turning the message into a comparison report.

Do not use:

- Static booking parity.
- Treatment-menu overlap.
- "Competitor X books better."
- A weak comparison added because a template has a blank.
- A competitor to manufacture urgency where no current signal exists.

Create or link a **Competitor Library** entry only when competitor activity is actually used. Do not use **Competitor Library - Batch02**.

## 8. Draft Touch 1

Rules:

- US English.
- 45 to 70 words excluding subject (greeting and signature included) and 5 to 8 sentences in the body (the question counts), per the five rules in section 8b. The word limit binds first.
- Order: introduction and credibility, signal, handoff, how we can help, one question.
- Founder voice, not AE voice (section 8b).
- No em dash.
- One question, at the end.
- No competitor sentence by default.
- No modeled treatments, appointments, slots, memberships, hours or money.
- No meeting, call, trial or asset offer.
- No generic compliment, fake familiarity or jargon. A slightly conversational tone is preferred, and one "I noticed" in the opening signal sentence is acceptable, provided every fact stated stays observed and sourced.
- State observed facts only. Use the final question to test the inferred objective.

**Subject:** `[Company] / [current situation]`

```text
[Name],

I'm Soban, founder of KodoAI. We've worked with [two or three names from the approved client list].

[Company] [verified, time-bound signal]. [Specific customer or operational task] still [observed handoff stated without interpretation].

We build custom AI systems around [vector-specific outcome]. [Optional, only if the email stays within 70 words: I think we could [how that applies to the observed handoff].]

Is [specific objective connected to the signal] something you're actively working on this quarter?

Soban | Founder, KodoAI
```

Vector-specific outcome lines:

| Vector | Wording |
|---|---|
| Conversion recovery | the handoff between an inquiry landing and someone actually booking |
| Capacity | keeping rising booking and follow-up volume from becoming more front-desk work |
| Capacity (only for a verified after-hours route failure) | handling and booking calls that land after the front desk closes |
| Rebooking and retention | bringing due clients back on treatment cadence without manual chasing |

The wording may be tightened for the prospect, but the logical order remains: **introduction and credibility → signal → handoff → how we can help → objective question**.

### 8b. The five rules and the credibility line

Source: Soban's reference “5 Rules for Good Cold Emails,” adapted for KodoAI. These govern the order and length of Touch 1 only. Every evidence, claim and offer restriction elsewhere still applies.

| # | Rule | How it applies to a KodoAI Touch 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 to 8 sentences, max | Count the body only, not the subject, greeting or signature; the question counts. The Touch 1 limit of 45 to 70 words excluding subject (greeting and signature included) also applies and binds first, so write about five short sentences of roughly 12 words each. |
| 2 | Lead with who you are and why you're worth talking to | Open with “I'm Soban, founder of KodoAI.” and one sentence naming enterprise clients, such as “We've worked with Rolls-Royce, Saudi Aramco and Patagonia.” Do not mention Bluegile. The prospect's signal comes second. |
| 3 | Personalize. But stay on topic! | One sentence, two at most: the observed signal and the observed handoff. No compliment, rapport detail or other fact about the business. |
| 4 | Explain in 1-2 sentences how you think you can help them | “We build custom AI systems around [vector-specific outcome].” Add a second sentence beginning “I think we could...” only if the email stays within 70 words. No numbers, guarantees, savings or claims about unobserved performance. |
| 5 | Write like a founder, not an AE | First person, plain words, short sentences, contractions allowed. One question and no meeting or demo ask. Avoid “I wanted to reach out,” “I hope this finds you well,” “touch base,” “circle back,” “quick question,” “reach out,” “synergy,” “leverage,” “best-in-class,” “cutting-edge,” “game-changing,” “unlock,” “empower,” “seamless,” “turnkey,” “pain points” and “solutions” as a vague noun. |

Word budget for the whole email, excluding subject: greeting 1, introduction 5, credibility about 9, signal and handoff about 15 to 20, how we can help about 15, question about 15, signature 4. The relevance-led lane drops the signal and states the handoff only.

#### Credibility line (named clients)

Touch 1 does not mention Bluegile (Soban's decision). Credibility is the enterprise clients his team has worked with, written as “We've worked with” followed by two or three names. Every name must appear in a case study on Bluegile's published portfolio (https://www.bluegile.xyz/portfolio, read October 10, 2026). That page is the internal source; the email does not cite it.

| Names | What the case study says (internal only, not for the email) |
|---|---|
| Default: Rolls-Royce, Saudi Aramco, Patagonia | Rolls-Royce: IoT consulting on engine telemetry and data processing. Saudi Aramco: well-drilling progress reporting tool, staff augmentation. Patagonia: integration of e-commerce platforms with its ERP and 3PL providers. |
| Also approved, when they fit the handoff and the word limit | Almarai (WhatsApp chatbot and admin dashboard), Repligen (over five years of digital transformation work), Saudi Investment Bank, MagPaiAnalytics, TalisAI, Art of Education, Kbean, OrderCircle, AirTrail. |

Default sentence: “We've worked with Rolls-Royce, Saudi Aramco and Patagonia.” One sentence. Choose names a US med-spa owner will recognize.

Never state or imply:

- a med-spa, aesthetics or healthcare client;
- that any named company uses, endorses or referred KodoAI. Say “we've worked with,” never “our customers,” “trusted by” or “clients include”;
- what the work was beyond what the case study says, or any result, saving, rating or client count;
- names that appear on bluegile.xyz only as logos (Patreon, Nutrabolt, Jazzcash), unless Soban confirms them in chat for that send;

If a prospect asks, answer plainly that the work was done through Soban's parent company, Bluegile Technologies.

Re-read the portfolio before each new batch, and drop any name whose case study changes or disappears.
