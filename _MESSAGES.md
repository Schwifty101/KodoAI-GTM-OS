## Rules that keep the number alive
1. **Don't send identical text 51 times.** Even with batches, use the merge fields, and if your tool supports rotation, write 2 alternates per batch (swap the opener and the CTA line only).
2. **No links, no price, no attachments in message 1.** Price after they reply — the research is clear that PKR 15,000 anchors you as cheap.
3. **Opt-out line stays in.** It's your PECA Section 25 cover and it measurably lowers report rate.
4. **Stop instantly on "no".** A block costs you far more than a lead.
5. **Watch the ratio.** If blocks + reports exceed ~2–3% of sends, pause and fix targeting before continuing.
6. **Only promise examples you can actually send.** Several messages offer a 30-second clip or before/after — have those ready before you start the batch.

## Timing
Weekdays, 11am–1pm (pre-lunch) or 3–5pm (afternoon lull). Avoid Friday Jummah hours and 7–11pm dinner rush.

## Follow-up (per lead, stop on any reply)
- **Day 0** — batch message above
- **Day 2–3** — proof only, no ask: the 30-second clip or a screenshot
- **Day 5–6** — soft close + exit: "No issue if it's not the right time — want the free menu-photo tips doc anyway? I'll leave it there 🙏"
